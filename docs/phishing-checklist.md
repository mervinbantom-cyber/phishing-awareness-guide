# Before You Click: Checklist
-[ ] Do I know the sender?
-[ ] Is the email address spelled correctly?
-[ ] Am I being rushed or threatened?
-[ ] Does the link go where it says (hover to check)?
-[ ] Is an attachment unexpected?
-[ ] Is it asking for a password or personal details?

If you ticked "No" to the first two questions or "Yes" to any other, stop and report it.
