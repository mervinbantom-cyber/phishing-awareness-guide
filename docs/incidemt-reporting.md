# What To DO IF You Spot or Fall for Phishing
# If you spot a suspicious message
1. Do not click, reply or open attachments.
2. Report it to the SOC or IT helpdesk.
3. Delete the message.
## If you already clicked or shared details
1. Disconnect from the network if you can.
2. Change your passwords right away, starting with the affected account.
3. Turn on multi-factor authentication.
4. Tell the SOC or IT helpdesk immediately. Quick reporting limits the damage.
5. Watch your accounts for unusual activity.
## Information to include in your report
- Date and time you received the message
- Sender's address
- Subject line
- What you clicked or shared, if anything
- A screenshot (never forward a suspicious attachment)
