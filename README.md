# Phishing Awareness Guide
A short guide to help students and staff spot and report phishing emails.
## About
Phishing is one of the most common cyber threats. This project gives simple, practical advice, based on the "Think Before You Click"awareness campaign (Pause. Check. Protect.).
## Who is it for?
- Students and Staff
- Anyone who wants to stay safe online
## What's inside
- docs/phishing-red-flags.md: signs of a phishing message
- docs/phishing-checklist.md: a checklist to use before clicking
- docs/incident-reporting.md: what to do if you spot or fall for phishing
## How to use it
Read the red flags guide first, then keep the checklist handy.
